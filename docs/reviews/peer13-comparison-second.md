## What the peer adds, and what changes

1. **Perception/memory conclusion identity:** accepted new finding. Printed pp. 345–346 conclude reasonable belief in the same proposition or case state. Use `reasonable_believe/1` for both when the ground term denotes the identical proposition/circumstance. A generic property label is not a license to combine unrelated cases. A focused rename needs migration and two-scheme independent-support checks. It does not repair perception’s existing observer/context defect.
2. **Moral badness versus bad character:** the omitted vocabulary decision is real. The conclusion that they must be identical is stronger than the source establishes: entry 47 says moral badness; entry 42 says bad character. Preserve distinct predicates and explicitly document that a model must justify any bridge. Entry47 currently requires the moral judgment as a premise; merely adding a name alias would not make it infer moral badness from bias.
3. **`biased/1` inherited wording:** verify against the original imported Carneades file and restore its generic wording. This changes rendering/provenance, not inference.
4. **Source-role repairs already closed:** peer holds 31.3,31.4 and 31.5 for the missing quality/event roles. Our typed character and scoped event repairs plus wrong-context controls address these. The predicted future tendency in 31.4 must not be mistaken for a new trait assertion.

## What peer approval does not establish

The peer approved the baseline forms of 31.2,54.1,56 and60. Our independent source comparison found respectively the diagram/conclusion mix-up, a missing coextension condition, conditional exception-witness requirements, and cross-recaller undercutters. These now have source-supported repairs and independent adverse controls. Agreement on the final stage of a repaired record does not validate the earlier formula or fixture.

Eight peer-approved records remain qualified here: `cause_to_effect`, `sign`, `abductive_backward`, `circumstantial_ad_hominem`, `rule_physical_world_2`, `rule_mental_world`, `plea_for_excuse`, and `perception`. A fixed dialogue/context convention is a defensible bounded use, and these are not declared unusable. However, it does not prove that shared case terms, multiple effects, multiple candidate interpretations, or several arguments by one person are isolated. The peer provides no new evidence disposing of those specific collision risks. In particular, one pleader can still make two different pleas.

There is no unsupported original-source hold on `cause_to_effect`: the imported code cited no separate source, and the supplied compendium can serve as its declared source. Our consolidated adjudication already corrected that provenance distinction. No other second-half hold is retracted on PR13 evidence.

The new likelihood defaults for 33.1–33.2 and the rule-application exceptions for 55.1 close available-book question gaps. They do not certify the original 1995 work. Conversely, `ethotic2` has a discrepancy against the already available compendium regardless of whether the older work is later obtained: decreased credibility does not establish the contradictory proposition. The inherited slippery-slope pair remains a useful component decomposition rather than a complete instance of the full printed normative scheme.

## Release and plan implication

PR13’s statement that nothing besides publishing authorization stands in the way of R1 is not established by this comparison. Parent engine review independently found and repaired runtime defects; those lie outside this source-only comparison. Keep the exact reviewed baseline and repaired candidate distinct. The new structured review fields are a useful auditability improvement, but a named/date-stamped approval does not itself prove source fidelity, fixture adequacy or engine safety.

The next work is bounded: integrate the verified vocabulary/template correction with migration and independent support checks; preserve per-record source and context qualifications; refresh approval fingerprints only after accepting those changes; and use the unified acceptance gate on the final candidate. No additional broad voting round is needed. Do not publish or contact authors on the strength of this comparison.
